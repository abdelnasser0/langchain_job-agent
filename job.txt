Job Title: Junior-to-Mid AI & Machine Learning Engineer (GenAI & Agents)

Location: Cairo, Egypt (Hybrid – 3 days onsite)

Company: NileFlow Technologies

About the Role:

NileFlow Technologies is building autonomous workflow assistants for enterprise operations across the MENA region. We are looking for an AI & Machine Learning Engineer to help design, evaluate, and deploy multi-step LLM agents and computer vision pipelines into production.

Key Responsibilities:

Design and orchestrate stateful AI agent workflows using Python, LangChain, and LangGraph for document processing and customer support automation.

Fine-tune and evaluate transformer-based NLP and Computer Vision models for Arabic and English document extraction.

Build RESTful inference microservices and integrate vector databases for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

Containerize ML pipelines using Docker and deploy scalable endpoints on AWS SageMaker and Kubernetes (EKS).

Monitor model latency, token usage, and drift in production environments using automated CI/CD pipelines.

Required Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field (or final-year student with strong applied internship experience).

Strong proficiency in Python and deep learning frameworks (PyTorch or TensorFlow), with hands-on experience in CNNs, RNNs, and Transformers.

Practical experience building LLM applications or tool-calling agents using LangChain, LangGraph, or similar orchestration frameworks.

Familiarity with REST APIs, Git, and relational or NoSQL databases (e.g., PostgreSQL, Firebase, or MongoDB).

Strong analytical skills and ability to communicate technical trade-offs clearly in English.

Preferred / Nice-to-Have Qualifications (Intentional Gaps for Testing):

2+ years of full-time industry experience shipping production ML systems at scale.

Hands-on experience deploying and managing workloads on AWS (SageMaker, EKS) and Kubernetes.

Experience writing high-throughput backend services in Go or Rust.

Familiarity with Apache Spark or large-scale distributed data engineering pipelines.
